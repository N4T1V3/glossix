Modified for Glossix: converted StarDict to JSON, extracted translations and IPA, added identifiers and search fields. Some entries include multiple senses or incomplete translations. Imported data is unreviewed and is not every Italian word or inflected form. Attribution and ShareAlike apply to this dictionary data. The authored learning course is a separate collection.

Pronunciation update: original IPA is retained in the ipa field where supplied. Missing IPA was transcribed with the bundled eSpeak NG Italian voice. Displayed hints are approximate English sound guides derived from IPA, with capitals indicating stress.
