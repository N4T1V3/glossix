# japanese–English reference dictionary

Wiktionary contributors via DBnary and WikDict / Karl Bartel. Source: https://download.wikdict.com/dictionaries/stardict/wikdict-ja-en.zip . Edition metadata is preserved in japanese-source.ifo. Data: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .

Glossix modifications: converted StarDict to JSON, extracted meanings and IPA, added identifiers and language tags, omitted entries matching conservative profanity/usage checks. Missing translations are excluded from practice. Retained translations are unreviewed reference data, not exhaustive or guaranteed accurate. This derived dictionary remains subject to ShareAlike.

Published 13523 entries. Screening statistics: japanese-content-safety-report.json. Automated checks reduce unwanted content but do not replace editorial review. Original source archives are not bundled with this screened collection.
