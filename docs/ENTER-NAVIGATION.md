# Practice keyboard navigation — 1.5.23

Enter and clicking Continue share the existing button action in lesson recall, understanding/word-bank feedback, daily review, language spelling, English spelling, scenarios, lesson dialogue, visual-object answers and reading stories. After feedback, Continue receives focus. Before feedback, Enter in an answer field submits normally; Enter from the page body submits the current answer form or checks a word-bank phrase.

Enter from the page body can reveal a flashcard or move to the next pronunciation word. Flashcard ratings still require an explicit choice. Pronunciation is not advanced from the body while recording. Completion screens with multiple choices retain their individual buttons.

Held Enter cannot skip feedback or repeatedly submit. IME composition, modified Enter, open dialogs, unrelated search fields, selects, chat textareas and private notes are excluded. Existing Continue buttons remain available. No timed advancement or new rewards. No SQL change.

Tests cover eight feedback screens, button and Enter activation, held-key/IME/modifier protection, answer submission, flashcard/pronunciation navigation, dialog/chat/search isolation, and the complete reading-practice regression suite.
