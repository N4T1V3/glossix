# Numbers and grammatical gender

The 1.5.8 additions use original Glossix explanations and examples. Reference grammar and spelling checks:

- Russian: [Russian Lessons — number agreement](https://www.russianlessons.net/lessons/lesson11_main.php), [Gramota — numeral reference](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=spravka&query=%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B5%20%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B5).
- Italian: [Treccani — numerals](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/numerali_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [numeral spelling](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/numerali-prontuario_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/).
- Spanish: [RAE — numeral spellings](https://www.rae.es/diccionario-estudiante/docs/numerales.pdf), [FundéuRAE — neuter demonstratives](https://www.fundeu.es/recomendacion/los-demostrativos-no-necesitan-tilde/).
- French: [Académie française — number spelling](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/questions-de-langue), [vingt/cent agreement](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/absence-de-liaisons-apres-les-adjectifs-numeraux-cardinaux), [Lingolia — noun genders](https://francais.lingolia.com/en/grammar/nouns-and-articles).
- German: [Duden — hundert](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/hundert), [ein/eins](https://www.duden.de/sprachwissen/sprachratgeber/Ein-eins-unbestimmter-Artikel-oder-Zahlwort), [Lingolia — grammar](https://deutsch.lingolia.com/en/grammar).
- Brazilian and European Portuguese: [Ciberdúvidas — numeral gender](https://ciberduvidas-ql.iscte-iul.pt/consultorio/perguntas/a-variacao-em-genero-de-um-e-dois/35334), [numerals](https://ciberduvidas.iscte-iul.pt/consultorio/perguntas/a-definicao-de-numeral/21306), [demonstratives](https://ciberduvidas.iscte-iul.pt/consultorio/perguntas/isso--esse--isto--este/9168).

Scope: every cardinal from 0 to 100, practical hundreds and selected larger values up to one million, first–tenth ordinals, gender-sensitive number forms, common noun/article combinations, agreement phrases, fractions, percentages and decimals. This is a beginner expansion rather than an exhaustive account of declension or every number.

Russian and German have three noun genders. Italian, Spanish, French and Portuguese have masculine and feminine nouns. Spanish and Portuguese neuter demonstrative pronouns are taught separately; they do not create a neuter noun class. Brazilian and European Portuguese number spellings and train/currency examples remain distinct.
